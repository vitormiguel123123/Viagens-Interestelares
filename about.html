OUR THEME'S APPLICATIONS



Spacecraft Dragon 2 from the american company Spacex, docking with the International Space Station

Interstellar travel, even though it has yet to begin, is being influenced by humanity's ongoing orbital advancements, with various private companies—such as SpaceX, Blue Origin, and Virgin Galactic—already offering potential tickets to space.



Reusable sub-orbital ocket New Shepard, made and operated by Blue Origin.

Blue Origin Enterprises

is a private American space technology company headquartered in Kent, Washington. The company operates the reusable suborbital rocket New Shepard, designed specifically for space tourism; it is capable of vertical takeoffs and landings. Additionally, it can transport people and customer payloads on a suborbital trajectory.
Its first crewed flight took place in July 2021, with four crew members, including billionaire Jeff Bezos.
Its last flight occurred in January 2026, and the program has been on hold since then.
➤ Price of a trip: Approx.

US$ 450,000 or R$ 2,243,970



Super heavy lift semi-reusable launch vehicle Falcon 9, operated and made by SpaceX.

SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies)

is an American aerospace manufacturer and provider of space transportation, telecommunications, and artificial intelligence services, headquartered at the Starbase Development Complex in Kopernik Shores, Texas.
The company utilizes various rockets such as Starship and several others in the Falcon series, including the Falcon 9, which is responsible for launching the Dragon spacecraft—the inspiration for the creation of the more modern Dragon 2, which is itself launched by the Falcon 9 Block 5.
Its first crewed flight took place in 2021 as the SpaceX Crew-1 mission, participating in Expedition 64 to the International Space Station, with its latest flight occurring in February 2026 as SpaceX Crew-16.
➤ Price of a trip:

~US$ 55 million per seat, or R$ 274 million



Suborbital Spaceplane Model 339 SpaceShipTwo, made by The Spaceship Company and operated by Virgin Galactic.

Virgin Galactic Holdings

is a British-American spaceflight company founded by Richard Branson and the Virgin Group conglomerate.
Headquartered in California with launch operations in New Mexico, the company develops commercial spacecraft and offers suborbital spaceflights specifically for space tourism.
The company operates several aircraft designed for suborbital conditions, such as the White Knight Two, which is used to lift the crewed SS2—or SpaceShipTwo—known as the VSS Enterprise or VSS Unity.
The company's first crewed flight took place in October 2010 with the VSS Enterprise, which completed 40 flights before crashing in the Mojave Desert, California.
The other aircraft, VSS Unity, continues to conduct suborbital flights to this day.
➤ Price of a trip:

~US$750,000 per seat, or R$ 3,739,950
